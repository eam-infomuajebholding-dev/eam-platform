# Emmar Al Asala Wa Al Muasara - Engineering Consultancy Website

## Design Style
- **References**: Luxury engineering firm websites, dark navy & gold premium branding
- **Color Palette**: 
  - Primary: #1a1a2e (Deep Navy/Black)
  - Secondary: #c9a84c (Gold/Orange)
  - Accent: #e8d48b (Light Gold)
  - Background: #0d0d0d (Rich Black)
  - Text: #ffffff (White)
  - Muted: #a0a0a0 (Gray)
  - Light BG: #f5f5f5 (for contact form section)
- **Typography**: Serif for headings (Playfair Display), Sans-serif for body (Inter/Tajawal for Arabic)
- **Key Component Styles**: Gold gradients, subtle animations, glass-morphism cards, elegant borders with gold accents
